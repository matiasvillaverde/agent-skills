# Research basis

Reviewed 2026-11-15. Original synthesis; no upstream skill files or workflow framework
are bundled. Sources are evidence, not authority to override the user or repository.

- [Matt Pocock: grilling](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/5863806dd4e15c63174bee4e7515a4256792ecfa/skills/productivity/grilling/SKILL.md): resolve dependent decisions in order, research facts, and supply recommendations. `grill-me` and `grill-with-docs` are wrappers. We do not require every possible branch, mandatory researchers, leading “yes” phrasing or a final approval ritual.
- [Superpowers: brainstorming](https://github.com/obra/superpowers/blob/22f0b463e7a5e579904dd6211a520321035526f7/skills/brainstorming/SKILL.md): read context, discover the problem and challenge missing assumptions. We omit the universal creative-work trigger, obligatory design/plan gates, repeated playback and automatic committed documents.
- [Trail of Bits: historical clarification skill](https://github.com/trailofbits/skills/blob/c070b9b5881183ea5f6e320ff06c46688becb13e/plugins/ask-questions-if-underspecified/skills/ask-questions-if-underspecified/SKILL.md): prioritize essential unknowns and offer concrete options. That path was unavailable on current main; this is explicitly a historical reference. No extra confirmation after clear delegation.
- [GitHub Spec Kit: clarify](https://github.com/github/spec-kit/blob/2ee2b1e8005905db05f5717300a469b3068b908f/templates/commands/clarify.md): self-contained questions, rationale, material ambiguity and updating contradictory spec text. This is a command template, not a standalone skill. We omit its hooks, mandatory spec layout, five-question quota and five-word answer restriction.
- [Addy Osmani: interview-me](https://github.com/addyosmani/agent-skills/blob/39b723ceebf81474d0557c1fecd01eddab585563/skills/interview-me/SKILL.md): distinguish an outcome from the first suggested feature. We reject self-assigned confidence thresholds, intentionally leading guesses, rejecting “you decide” and forcing another turn after clear permission to proceed.
- [mthines: interview](https://github.com/mthines/agent-skills/blob/3cc1473b5e970b764587acdd22dc3b162c588e10/skills/analysis/interview/SKILL.md): research before asking, distinguish stated/inferred scope and skip unnecessary interviews. Its question-design/research-grounding references were also read. We use adaptive pacing instead of mandatory batching, a two-round cap or a new brief path for every task.
- [modakbul-gongbang: deep-interview](https://github.com/modakbul-gongbang/harness/blob/8a4d5df4f1fdf7a575d81e34f4fee6b4e758efd7/skills/deep-interview/SKILL.md): recognize unknown answers and adapt probing. We omit quantitative ambiguity scores, fixed rounds, mandatory interviewer agents and repeated questions about the interview process.

Primary interviewing and product-shaping sources are linked in
[research guidance](research.md). Repository comparisons establish instruction content,
not that one skill empirically outperforms another. Synthetic qualification checks local
behavior; actual user experience and different clients/models may reveal further gaps.
