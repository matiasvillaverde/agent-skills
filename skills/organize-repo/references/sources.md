The source search branched from organization skills to instruction discovery and then to
language-specific build/resource contracts. Most generic organizers were weaker than that
combination. These references support specific decisions, not a popularity ranking or an
exhaustive survey. The workflow's effectiveness still needs representative use; validation
of Markdown or frontmatter alone does not establish it.
