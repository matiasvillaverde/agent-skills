# Treasury and Cash Research

Compare attainable return with capital risk and access when money is needed. Interest
earned by a company and dividends it pays to an owner are different transactions.

## Establish the money and constraints

Identify personal versus company ownership, base/spending currencies, jurisdiction,
time horizon and tolerable loss or access delay. Use existing confirmed preferences and
records before asking questions. Keep the last reconciled balance/date distinct from
live bank cash; pending receipts and claims are not cash in the account.

Separate imminent payments, known liabilities/tax, operating reserves and genuinely
long-term surplus. Do not count liabilities twice or treat a transfer as new income or
expense. A reimbursement's invoice currency does not establish its settlement currency
or payment date. Temporary spending spikes and old budgets need an explicit forecast,
not mechanical annualisation. Ask only for missing facts that change the feasible choice;
continue the comparisons that do not depend on them.

## Compare actual products

Read the current product terms, fee schedule, legal-entity protection disclosure and exact
security/share-class document. Verify company/country eligibility, minimums, account plan,
funding currency, access/settlement and rate date. Advertised availability is not approval
of this customer's account. Use the available `finance-data` routes for original rates and
instrument identity, and [product distinctions](references/products.md) for comparison.

Show annualized rate and expected proceeds over the actual holding period separately.
Distinguish variable/fixed, gross/net, day-count, accrual/compounding and reinvestment
assumptions. Identify fees already embedded in a fund yield before deducting platform,
dealing, account and FX costs. Preserve illustrative versus executable quotes; a past
auction result is not today's brokerage purchase price.

Aggregate eligible deposits by depositor and legal bank, including current/savings balances
and accrued interest, with room for expected inflows. Separate deposit insurance, custody
segregation and investor compensation; the latter do not insure ordinary market losses.
Verify actual jurisdiction, scheme, eligibility and exclusions before quoting protection.

## Stress the choice

Consider payment timing, redemption/provider interruption, rate rises and falls, credit
stress, inflation and FX. Match maturities to cash needs; do not assume an early sale at
par. A fund can be liquid in ordinary conditions and face stress restrictions. Maintain
independent payment access where the interruption would matter.

Compare foreign-currency returns in the spending currency and explain hedging costs and
residual risks where applicable. A higher USD yield does not guarantee a higher EUR return.
Long nominal bonds, equities and gold require a loss-tolerant investment allocation;
they are not interchangeable with payment reserves.

## Deliver a reviewable recommendation

Present the supported shortlist, net-cost calculations, liquidity/risk trade-offs and the
facts still needed to select an amount or execute. Separate tax assumptions from verified
rules; use current official jurisdictional guidance when tax changes the result. Do not
infer tax residence or worldwide tax treatment from incorporation or a six-month rule.

Keep research and private balance evidence in the existing owner location. Opening or
funding an account, moving money, purchasing securities or posting accounting entries
requires the relevant task authorization and owning workflow; a research recommendation
does not perform or authorize those actions.
