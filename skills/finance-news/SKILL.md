# Financial News and Catalysts

Find what changed, verify it, and explain its possible financial relevance. A headline,
sentiment score or coincident price move does not establish a cause or a trade.

Establish coverage, exact time window and timezone from the request. Distinguish overnight,
premarket, regular session and post-close news. For historical briefs, exclude later
knowledge. Keep event time, initial publication, corrections/updates and retrieval separate.

Use available search/news tools and existing research to discover developments; read
the original release, filing, regulator decision, transcript or original reporting.
Check current tool parameters instead of assuming a provider supports news/date filters.
Trace syndicated reports to the underlying source and preserve unresolved access gaps.

Prioritize developments that could change revenues, margins, financing, regulation,
ownership or the investment thesis. Distinguish confirmed events, attributed reports,
rumors, management plans and scheduled future events. A scheduled release is not a result;
an announcement is not completed execution. Check corrections and conflicting accounts.

For earnings or macro surprises, compare with a dated expectation that existed before
the release. Company guidance, analyst consensus and our estimate are different baselines.
If the consensus source is missing, report actuals/guidance without inventing a beat/miss.

When explaining a price move, establish the actual instrument, session and measurement
window, including split/dividend effects. Compare the relevant benchmark/sector and
contemporaneous alternatives. Describe causal links as hypotheses unless supported;
the most visible headline need not explain the whole move. Source uncertainty and market
uncertainty are separate. Use `finance-data`, `finance-company` or `finance-macro` when
available and when those deeper questions matter, without expanding every news brief.

Deliver a concise dated account: what happened, original evidence, observed reaction,
why it could matter, what remains uncertain and the next meaningful event. A table can
separate event time, source and interpretation. Say when the search found no material
news within its coverage; do not claim nothing happened everywhere.

For recurring research, update the existing event/thesis log and retain corrections,
source dates and pending catalysts. Do not turn source tone into an invented numerical
probability or conviction score. Save private watchlists and licensed text privately.
This skill produces research; it does not send a brief, set up monitoring, subscribe to
feeds or place trades unless separately instructed through the owning workflow.
